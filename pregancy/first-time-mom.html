First-Time Mom Guide

Becoming a mother for the first time brings excitement and plenty of questions. Use this step-by-step roadmap to stay organized from your first positive test to delivery day.

1. Early Prenatal Checkups

Register early at your local Barangay Health Station or OB-GYN clinic for blood pressure screening, ultrasound, and iron/folate supplements.

2. Balanced Nutrition & Hydration

Eat iron-rich greens, lean protein, calcium, and whole grains. Drink 8–10 glasses of water daily and avoid raw seafood or unpasteurized dairy.

3. Hospital Bag Checklist (Week 34)

Pack valid IDs, PhilHealth/health records, comfortable front-button clothes, maternity pads, baby onesies, diapers, and receiving blankets.

4. Emotional & Postpartum Care

Rest whenever possible and ask family or our on-duty nurse for help if you feel overwhelmed or experience persistent sadness after birth.
