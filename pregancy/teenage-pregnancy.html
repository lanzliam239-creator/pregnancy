Youth Care & Support

Teenage Pregnancy Support

If you are a teenager facing an unplanned or early pregnancy, you deserve compassionate, confidential medical care without judgment. Early prenatal support protects both your health and your baby's future.

Why Early Checkups Matter Most

Teenagers have higher risks for anemia and high blood pressure during pregnancy. Regular checkups and iron supplements keep both you and your baby safe.

Confidential Nurse Consultation

Not ready to visit a clinic alone? Use our Messages tab to talk directly with Nurse Clarissa Reyes, RN for private guidance.

Your Right to Education

Schools and Alternative Learning Systems (ALS) provide flexible modules and support so young mothers can finish high school or college.
